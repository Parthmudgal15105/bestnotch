FAQ

Does it work without a notch?

Yes. On Macs and displays without a notch, BestNotch shows a slim pill centered in the menu bar.

Which music apps are supported?

Apple Music and Spotify. Other players and browsers aren't supported yet.

Will clearing the shelf delete my files?

No. The shelf only keeps references. Removing an item never touches the original.

Does it work in full-screen apps?

By default the island hides in full-screen apps; important activities like a finished timer still appear. You can change this in Settings.
