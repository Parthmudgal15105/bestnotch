Found a bug or have a question? Open an issue on GitHub. Please include your macOS version, your Mac model, and what you expected to happen.

Common questions

How do I open the island?

Hover over or click the notch area at the top of your screen, or press ⌥⌘N. Press Esc to close it.

The island doesn’t appear on my external display.

Open Settings › General › Displays and choose “All displays”, and make sure the display isn’t switched off in the list below it.

Now Playing doesn’t show my music.

BestNotch supports Music and Spotify. For artwork and controls, allow BestNotch in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Automation. Other players and browsers aren’t supported.

Clipboard history is empty.

Clipboard history is off until you turn it on in the Clipboard tab. If macOS asks, allow BestNotch to read the pasteboard (System Settings › Privacy & Security › Paste from Other Apps).

Will removing files from the Shelf delete them?

No. The Shelf only keeps references. Your original files are never moved, changed or deleted.

How do I uninstall BestNotch?

Quit BestNotch from its menu bar icon, then move the app to the Trash. Its data lives in its own container in ~/Library/Containers and can be deleted too.

Requirements

macOS 14 Sonoma or later, Apple silicon or Intel.
